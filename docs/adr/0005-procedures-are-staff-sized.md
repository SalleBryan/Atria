# 0005. Procedures are staff-sized

Status: accepted
Source: AD-05 in the Technical Document (ATR-TD-001, section 3)

## Decision

A patient can request a procedure, which creates a REQUESTED appointment with no time held.
Staff size and place it, and the patient confirms.

# 0001. Three service lines

Status: accepted
Source: AD-01 in the Technical Document (ATR-TD-001, section 3)

## Decision

General consultation, specialist consultation and procedure are booked differently. Check-up and
follow-up are appointment types inside a line, not lines of their own.

# 0011. Ratings stay off prices and off names

Status: accepted
Source: AD-11 in the Technical Document (ATR-TD-001, section 3)

Constraints: C-02 professional conduct and fees

## Decision

Feedback is collected after attended visits and seen by the institution, never shown on a
patient-facing clinician listing. Fees have a per-practice baseline. The ratingAffectsFee flag
exists and is refused by the Cameroon region pack.

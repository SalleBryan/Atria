# 0013. Region pack

Status: accepted
Source: AD-13 in the Technical Document (ATR-TD-001, section 3)

Constraints: C-03 personal data law

## Decision

Currency, phone rules, languages, timezone, holidays, address and ID formats, SMS sender policy,
emergency number and default fees come from a region pack chosen at onboarding and locked after
the first appointment. Cameroon: XAF, +237, French and English, Africa/Douala.

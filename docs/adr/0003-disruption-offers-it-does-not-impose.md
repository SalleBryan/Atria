# 0003. Disruption offers, it does not impose

Status: accepted
Source: AD-03 in the Technical Document (ATR-TD-001, section 3)

Constraints: C-01 staffing

## Decision

When a clinician is marked unavailable, affected specialist appointments move to DISRUPTED and
the patient is offered the next matching slots with one tap to confirm. Opt-in auto-accept
applies to same-day slots only.
